# DareMe — Pitch Deck Summary (10-Slide Outline)

---

### Slide 1: Cover
- **Title:** DareMe ⚡
- **Tagline:** Drop a dare. Take the challenge. Prove yourself.
- **Category:** Social Web3 Challenge Protocol
- **Ecosystem:** Botchain Testnet (Chain ID: 968)

---

### Slide 2: The Problem
- Traditional challenge platforms are centralized, ad-polluted, and gatekept.
- Most Web3 social experiments require financial tokens, complex staking, or speculative friction.
- Communities lack a simple, lightweight way to initiate and track friendly social challenges on-chain.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution
- **DareMe:** A zero-custody, decentralized dApp where challenges live directly on the blockchain.
- No database, no accounts, no tokens—just pure peer-to-peer engagement powered by smart contracts.

---

### Slide 4: Key Features
1. **Drop a Dare:** Anyone can author a challenge in categories like Fitness, Gaming, Knowledge, or Fun.
2. **Accept a Challenge:** Community members claim open dares in real-time.
3. **Prove & Complete:** Challengers report completion with on-chain timestamps.
4. **Live Explorer Integration:** Every interaction links directly to BohrScan.

---

### Slide 5: Target Audience
- **Web3 Communities & DAOs:** Engaging members with mini-quests and community activities.
- **Gamers & Creators:** Setting speedrun challenges, trivia, and competitive dares.
- **Botchain Testnet Users:** Generating authentic user activity and testing network capabilities.

---

### Slide 6: Product Architecture
- **Blockchain Layer:** Botchain Testnet (`https://rpc.bohr.life`, ID: 968)
- **Smart Contract:** Solidity 0.8.24 (`DareMe.sol`) verified on BohrScan
- **Client Application:** Next.js (App Router), Wagmi v2, Viem, Tailwind CSS
- **Infrastructure:** 100% decentralized state storage (zero centralized backend)
